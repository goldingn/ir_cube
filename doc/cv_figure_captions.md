Numbers are from the final run (#35). Sources: `outputs/cv_level_skill.csv`, `outputs/cv_level_skill_by_insecticide.csv`, `outputs/cv_n_star.csv`, `outputs/cv_change_skill.csv`.

**Figure (`figures/CV_variance_explained.png`).**
Accuracy of bioassay-based estimates (green; paler for the nearest bioassay, further away) and of the models (blue: *mechanistic only*, the dynamical model; magenta: *full model*, the two-stage model) for the level of mortality in single held-out bioassays, in space (top) and ahead in time (bottom), with local data and without it.
Each bar is the % of variance explained, 100 (1 − MSE / Var(y)). (A) The interpolation test assays, predicted by the full-data fit, which included them: a prediction at a location with a bioassay. (B) The same assays, held out: no training data at their pixels, median 43 km from the nearest bioassay; 992 assays at 89 pixels. (C) Assays held out in contiguous sub-national blocks of six countries (two folds pooled), median 161 km from the nearest bioassay; 5,355 assays at 578 pixels. A and B score the same assays, so the step from A to B changes only whether the model saw the local data. (D) The assays of 2014–2018, predicted from the data before 2014: 9,205 assays at 1,441 pixels, median 49 km and 3 years from the nearest bioassay. (E) The same for 2018–2022 from the data before 2018: 4,431 assays at 771 pixels, median 36 km and 2 years.
Bars. *Local bioassay* (A): the expected score of a second, independent bioassay at the same pixel-year (100 − 2 × the noise share), computed from the bioassay overdispersion ρ̂. *Nearest bioassay*: the single nearest training bioassay of the same insecticide in the same or the previous year; in D and E, where no training data exist in the forecast years, in the last two years before the origin (2012–2013 and 2016–2017), so it forecasts by persistence. The distances and years in the panel titles are to this bioassay. The models' bars score the map: the posterior mean of population mortality (for the two-stage model, without its site-level noise terms u and p).
The key labels the parts of a bar: explained variance (coloured; the white line is the estimate and the lighter area its 95% interval), unexplained variance (dark grey) and bioassay noise (light grey), which no prediction of population mortality can explain. The dotted line between them is the score of a perfect prediction of population mortality, given the target's beta-binomial bioassay noise at ρ̂: 76.3% (A, B), 77.1% (C), 80.0% (D) and 81.3% (E).
n\*<sub>CV</sub>, the number of bioassays from the same pixel-year whose mean matches a bar's accuracy, is (100 − ceiling) / (ceiling − score): two-stage model 1.10 (B), 0.68 (C), 0.40 (D) and 0.48 (E); dynamical model 0.85, 0.58, 0.36 and 0.43; nearest bioassay 0.68, 0.39, 0.35 and 0.38; in sample (A), two-stage 2.75 and dynamical 1.11. Supplementary Table [S?] gives n\*<sub>CV</sub> per insecticide for B and C.
Intervals are 95% intervals from 2,000 bootstrap resamples of pixels; each resample also takes a posterior draw of ρ by insecticide, which enters the dotted lines and the local bar. Dotted lines are drawn at their point estimates: 69.3–80.7% (A, B), 74.8–79.0% (C), 78.4–81.5% (D), 79.2–83.1% (E).

**Figure (`figures/CV_in_sample_pooled.png`).**
In-sample accuracy of the models against 1–3 independent bioassays at the location, pooled, for level (A) and change (B). The bars and key are as in the main figure; the greens darken with the number of bioassays.
(A) The assays of panel A of the main figure, as % of variance explained. The bioassay bars are the expected score of the mean of 1, 2 or 3 independent bioassays at the held-out bioassay's pixel-year, each of its size: 100 (1 − (1 + 1/n) F / Var(y)), with F the data floor; 52.5%, 64.4% and 68.3%, against 54.8% (mechanistic only) and 67.6% (full model).
(B) Skill against no change: 100 (1 − Σ(P − A)² / ΣA²) (the persistence index; Kitanidis & Bras 1980). The target A is the change in mortality between a pair of single bioassays of one insecticide at one pixel, one in the five years before a forecast origin (2014 or 2018) and one in the five years after, at least 3 years apart, divided by the years between them; every prediction P of it is divided by the same gap. A pair entering at both origins is scored once. Each pixel-insecticide has equal weight. 0 is as accurate as assuming no change; below 0 is less accurate. 8,264 pairs (65% of all pairs; median gap 4 years) at 1,607 pixel-insecticides in 584 pixels. The bioassay bars are the expected score of the mean of 1, 2 or 3 independent pairs at the pixel, each with the target pair's noise over a gap drawn from the targets' gaps: −13.0%, 15.0% and 24.4%, against 9.6% (mechanistic only) and 20.8% (full model). The ceiling, 43.1% (36.2–49.2%), is low: a single pair of bioassays carries little information about change. A single pair scores below 0: it is right on average, but its large readings overstate the change.
Intervals as in the main figure.
