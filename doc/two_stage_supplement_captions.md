# Draft captions: two-stage model components (R/fig_two_stage_components.R)

**Figure S1 (`figures/two_stage/supp_components_realisations.png`).**
Components of the two-stage model for deltamethrin at three pixels, as three joint posterior realisations (colours matched across rows). (A) A pixel with data before and after the net scale-up. (B) A pixel 409 km from the nearest deltamethrin assay. (C) The pixel with the largest fitted correction (ω + ξ in 2024) among pixels with three or more years of data. Rows, top to bottom: modelled LLIN use; the dynamical model's logit mortality m (black: posterior mean); annual anomalies η; their cumulative sum ξ = Ση, with ξ = 0 in 1995; the static field ω; the prediction target m + ω + ξ on the logit scale; and mortality, with observed bioassays (grey, area proportional to mosquitoes tested). Each realisation pairs one dynamical-model posterior draw with a draw of the correction from its Gaussian approximation, shifted for that dynamical draw (cut posterior). The pixel-year effect u and the pixel effect p are observation-level noise in the fitted model, and are not shown. Grey shading: projection beyond the last data year (2024), where η is simulated forward from its AR(1) model.

**Figure S2 (`figures/two_stage/supp_components_intervals.png`).**
Predicted susceptibility to deltamethrin at the three pixels of Figure S1: dynamical model alone (grey) and two-stage model (blue). Lines are posterior means and bands are 95% intervals from 2000 draws. The two-stage intervals include the dynamical model's uncertainty (through the cut-posterior shift), ω and ξ. They exclude observation-level noise: u, p and the assay-level (beta-binomial) noise. So they are for the population's mortality in each pixel-year, not for a new bioassay. Points: observed bioassays, with area proportional to mosquitoes tested. Lower panels: modelled LLIN use. Grey shading: projection beyond 2024.
